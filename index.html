So, What Good is International Relations?

Through many theories, the world's politics can be defined.

International relations help to define global politics. IR's various theories, like realism, liberalism, and constructivism (discussed above), help to ensure that the world does not descend into chaos and anarchy (mutually exclusive ideas). Without defining concepts like realism, liberalism, and constructivism, the world would have no meaning behind every political action.

In my opinion, I believe that international relations is crucial to the way that the world functions. Organizations like the United Nations rely on these theories to make decisions on the proceedings of the world. I think that realism, liberalism, and constructivism are the big three theories in IR, however, each have many sub-categories like structural realism.

I would also like to mention the importance of each of the image theories. 1st, 2nd, and 3rd image theory help to describe the larger conflicts and global proceedings. Each image theory can tie into other theories like realism directly when they are being used in the world.

Why Should People Care?

People should care about International Relations because it helps them understand the functioning of the global order. By understanding how International Relations work, a person can be better equipped to look at the world's decisions and understand the choices that are being made. Through IR theories, people are able to learn why big wars happen instead of just accepting them.

What Does the Future Hold?

The future of International Relations is not one fixed idea because more theories might come about. Looking at the past we can see this, through constructivism appearing after realism and liberalism in the time of the Cold War. International Relations will always have a part in the way the world functions and without it the world would descend into anarchy and/or chaos.
